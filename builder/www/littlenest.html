How estimates work
Weekly rates are shown as entered. Daily and hourly rates are multiplied by your sessions per week and hours per session. Monthly estimates are annualised using 52 weeks ÷ 12 months. If you enter weeks per year, that figure is used for the annual estimate. These are arithmetic comparisons based on your entries, not nursery fee checks or funding calculations.

Privacy in this prototype

This standalone MVP preview stores the profile and nursery quotes you enter in your browser’s local storage. The information does not sync to a server.
